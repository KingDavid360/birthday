Dear Antoinette,

Birthdays are funny things, they are supposed to be about the person celebrating, but all they have done for me is make me grateful that June 13th exists.

There are people you meet and forget by Tuesday. And then there are people who rearrange something quietly inside you, the kind that makes you consider the person you want to be, the kind that, when you remember them, a small smile just appears on your face without asking for permission.

I am not going to say anything dramatic as usual 😂 😂, but I will be honest, before I knew you, I was drawn to you. You know how I always tell you that you are such a sweet soul, I kind of already knew that before getting to know you. I do not know how. But getting to experience this soul has made me want to be present for every June 13th of yours, for as long as you will have me.

With genuine love 💕

"You make me wanna boogieeee 😂 😂 😂"

Happy Birthday, Antoinette. 🌸
